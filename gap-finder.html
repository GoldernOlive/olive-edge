🎯 Gap Finder
Tracked POD niches ranked by opportunity — blending weekly demand signals with live Etsy supply and incumbent seller strength.
Loading…
How it works: Each niche is tracked weekly — blending Google autocomplete depth, Reddit buyer mentions, and Etsy favourite counts into a demand signal, then weighing that against listing volume and how strongly the top sellers are established. Gap score = how much buyer interest is left once competition is accounted for. A niche can have high interest but a low gap if it's crowded — that's why the two numbers differ.
