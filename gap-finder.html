🎯 Gap Finder
Products people are searching for but can't find on Etsy. High Google signal + low listing count = your window to list first.

How it works: Queries Google Autocomplete UK for 20 POD search terms → finds what buyers are actually searching → checks how many Etsy listings exist for each → ranks by Gap Score (demand ÷ supply). High score = few sellers, real demand = list now.
